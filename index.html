Privacy Policy for Retro Snake Game

Last Updated: November 16, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Retro Snake Game ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Our app collects the following types of information:

2.1 Game Data (Stored Locally)

  • Lives Count: Number of lives you have remaining
  • Life Cooldown Timer: Time until your next life regenerates
  • Game Progress: Your current game state and score

This data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Advertising ID

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Information

  • Game Data: Used solely to maintain your game progress and lives system
  • Advertising Data: Used by Google AdMob to show relevant advertisements and measure ad performance

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (see their privacy policy above)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not operate any servers that store your data
  • Your data is only accessible on your device
  • Uninstalling the app will delete all locally stored data

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is designed to be appropriate for all ages. Advertising shown through AdMob is filtered to comply with children's privacy laws.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete Your Data: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Adjust your device settings to limit ad tracking
  • Access Your Data: All your data is stored locally on your device

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For advertisement delivery
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

10. Consent

By using Retro Snake Game, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
